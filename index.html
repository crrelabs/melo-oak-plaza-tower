Luxury Living in the
Health District

Oak Plaza Tower is a luxury rental community at 1415 NW 15 Avenue in Miami’s Health District. Residences are finished with granite countertops, European-style cabinets, laminated wood floors in living rooms, porcelain tiles in bathrooms, and full-size washer and dryer machines.

A few blocks from Miami’s medical campus and five minutes from Downtown, you are close to Civic Center Station, loanDepot park, the expressways, and South Beach. A large swimming pool, fully equipped fitness center, covered parking, and private terraces complete the lifestyle.

Features & Amenities

Everything you need for the Miami lifestyle — at home in the Health District, minutes from Downtown.

Large Swimming Pool

Lounge poolside under the Miami sun. A large swimming pool and sundeck sit at the base of the tower, framed by palms — an easy unwind after a day in the city.

Fully Equipped Fitness Center

Stay on your routine without leaving the building. The fully equipped fitness center has cardio, free weights, and strength machines — with pool and palm views through floor-to-ceiling windows.

Designer Lobby

An art-filled lobby lounge sets the tone at the door — a gallery wall, contemporary seating, and a polished welcome home after work in the Health District or Downtown.

Covered Parking & Access

A covered parking garage with security remote-control access keeps your commute simple — three minutes to I-95, SR 836, and the rest of Miami.
